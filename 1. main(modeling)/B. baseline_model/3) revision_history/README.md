# Baseline revision history

## What is recorded here

| Snapshot | Main content | Difference to keep in mind |
|---|---|---|
| 2026-09-21 | City entry/fleet objective plus an expanded decomposition of unmet demand into human-taxi switching and other losses | Includes extra outcome variables and feasibility ideas beyond the compact baseline |
| 2026-09-30 | Compact entry + fleet + expected surplus/shortage formulation | Describes $M$ as ride-hailing opportunities; the current English formulation broadens it to all-mode potential travel opportunities |

The common structure is $D_c^s=M_cp_c^s$ and $\kappa_cq_c-D_c^s=O_c^s-U_c^s$, with binary entry, integer fleets, city caps, and a total fleet cap. The interpretation of the denominator $M$ changes what $p$ means, even if the algebra remains the same.

These PDFs are historical model specifications, not additional experiment results. Use the current formulation in `1) baseline` when matching equations to the current notebooks.

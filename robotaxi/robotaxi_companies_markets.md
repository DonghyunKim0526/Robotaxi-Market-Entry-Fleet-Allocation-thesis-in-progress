# U.S. Robotaxi Companies and Operating Markets

![U.S. Robotaxi Markets by Company](./미국_로봇택시_시장_현황.png)

## 1. Waymo

- **Current operating areas:** Atlanta(Georgia), Austin(Texas), Dallas(Texas), Houston(Texas), Los Angeles(California), Miami(Florida), Nashville(Tennessee), Orlando(Florida), Phoenix(Arizona), San Antonio(Texas), San Francisco Bay Area(California)

- **Future candidate operating areas:** Baltimore(Maryland), Boston(Massachusetts), Charlotte(North Carolina), Chicago(Illinois), Denver(Colorado), Detroit(Michigan), Las Vegas(Nevada), Minneapolis(Minnesota), New Orleans(Louisiana), New York City(New York), Philadelphia(Pennsylvania), Pittsburgh(Pennsylvania), Portland(Oregon), Sacramento(California), San Diego(California), Seattle(Washington), St. Louis(Missouri), Tampa(Florida), Washington, D.C.(District of Columbia)

- **Source:** [Waymo — Where Waymo Operates](https://waymo.com/rides/)

---

## 2. Zoox

- **Current operating areas:** Las Vegas(Nevada), San Francisco(California)

- **Future candidate operating areas:** Atlanta(Georgia), Austin(Texas), Los Angeles(California), Miami(Florida)

- **Source:** [Zoox — Where to Ride](https://zoox.com/where-to-ride)

> **Note:** Zoox lists all six cities on its official “Where to Ride” page, but the service stages differ by city. Las Vegas and San Francisco are treated here as current public-rider markets, while Austin, Miami, Atlanta, and Los Angeles are classified as announced expansion or rollout markets.

---

## 3. Tesla Robotaxi

- **Current operating areas:** Austin(Texas), Dallas(Texas), Houston(Texas)

- **Future candidate operating areas:** No additional U.S. cities have been confirmed in a sufficiently clear, city-by-city official list.

- **Sources:**
  - [Tesla — Robotaxi](https://www.tesla.com/robotaxi)
  - [Reuters — Tesla expands Robotaxi service to Dallas and Houston](https://www.reuters.com/business/autos-transportation/tesla-rolls-out-robotaxis-dallas-houston-2026-04-18/)
  - [Reuters — Tesla rolls out unsupervised Robotaxis in the Austin Metro area](https://www.reuters.com/business/autos-transportation/tesla-rolls-out-unsupervised-robotaxis-austin-2026-06-03/)

> **Note:** Tesla has expressed a broad intention to expand Robotaxi service across additional U.S. markets, but unlike Waymo, it does not currently provide a reliable official “Up Next” city list. Therefore, unconfirmed cities are excluded.

# Related location and allocation problems

## Location decisions under operational uncertainty

| Paper | Main model / finding | Connection to robotaxi entry |
|---|---|---|
| Resource distribution under disease-spread uncertainty | Location and distribution decisions balance deployment/shipping/storage, demand coverage, and equity; compares SP and moment-based DRO | Shows how expected-cost and worst-case-service objectives can favor different plans; useful for future coverage/fairness constraints |
| Spaceport facility location | Selects candidate sites and launch allocations while accounting for population, trajectories, and air-traffic impacts; sensitivity examines rerouting costs | Demonstrates that physical feasibility and external operational impacts must accompany fixed-cost site selection |

The disease-resource study reports a cost/service trade-off: DRO is attractive when worst-case unmet demand is prioritized, while SP can offer an intermediate budget-conscious plan. The spaceport study links site decisions to mission allocation and operational restrictions rather than demand regression.

These are structural analogies, not robotaxi demand or cost calibration sources. Their application-specific equity, trajectory, shipping, and storage constraints are not present in the current robotaxi baseline.

## Documents and their contents

- **[01_Resource Distribution Inder Spatiotemporal Uncertainty of Disease Spread.pdf](01_Resource%20Distribution%20Inder%20Spatiotemporal%20Uncertainty%20of%20Disease%20Spread.pdf)** — Facility/resource allocation under epidemic uncertainty; compares stochastic and moment-DRO plans on cost, coverage, and equity.
- **[02_Spaceport Facility Location Planning.pdf](02_Spaceport%20Facility%20Location%20Planning.pdf)** — Facility location and mission allocation with population, trajectories, and air-traffic impacts; sensitivity to rerouting cost and operational restrictions.

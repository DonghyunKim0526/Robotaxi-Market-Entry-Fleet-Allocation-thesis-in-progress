# Sequential Charging Station Location Optimization under Uncertain Charging Behavior and User Growth

## One-Sentence Summary

This paper studies where and when to build electric vehicle charging stations over multiple years when future demand growth is uncertain and users choose stations probabilistically based on distance, station type, and home charging options.

## 1. Big Picture

This paper is about sequential infrastructure planning.

The planner does not build all charging stations at once. Instead, the planner decides where and what type of charging stations to build over several years.

The difficulty is that future EV charging demand is uncertain, and users do not simply choose the nearest station deterministically. Their choices depend on multiple factors.

The paper combines:

- multi-stage stochastic programming;
- EV demand growth uncertainty;
- multinomial logit user choice model;
- charging station location decisions;
- station type decisions;
- efficient reformulation methods.

## 2. Correct Classification

This paper is best understood as a multi-stage stochastic programming paper.

It is not primarily a DRO paper because it does not use a Wasserstein ball or worst-case distribution.

It is not an ILO or decision-focused learning paper because it does not learn a prediction model end-to-end through decision loss.

If connected to the contextual optimization taxonomy, it is closest to a predict-then-optimize or SLO-style structure:

generate demand scenarios
→ solve stochastic optimization problem

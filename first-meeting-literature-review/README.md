# First Meeting Literature Review

This folder summarizes the references shared by Professor Siqian Shen after our first meeting.

The goal of this review is not to fully reproduce every mathematical detail, but to understand the high-level motivation, modeling ideas, methodological contributions, and possible research directions.

## Goals

1. Understand the motivation behind optimization under multi-sourced data.
2. Summarize the key ideas of distributionally robust optimization.
3. Understand how contextual information can be used in stochastic optimization.
4. Identify possible research questions for future discussion.
5. Connect these topics with my background in applied statistics, machine learning, and military logistics.

## Organization

### `papers/`

Individual notes for each paper or reference.

### `synthesis/`

Higher-level reflections across papers, including common themes, possible research questions, and my potential contribution as an applied statistics student.

### `meeting_notes/`

Notes from meetings and follow-up discussions.

# Demand-variable review: changes and reasons

This folder preserves the marked-up demand-variable review. The changes concern **additional evidence, the interpretation of candidate variables, and consolidation of the working document**.

## Content additions recorded on 2026-10-01

| Part modified | What was added or clarified | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Automated-taxi choice studies | Added discussion of fare, waiting time, in-vehicle features, customer reviews, and the corresponding choice outcomes | Links candidate choice mechanisms to the service conditions actually studied, rather than treating them as observed city demand |
| Acceptance and usage-experience studies | Added survey-based acceptance mechanisms and distinctions between operating-area exposure, individual experience, and actual usage | Separates attitudes and experience measures from a directly observed robotaxi choice probability; avoids assuming a uniform positive experience effect |
| Supporting literature, papers 8–12 | Added SAV adoption with pilot experience, SAV preferences, AV preferences, a stated-choice review, and a pilot-user/ridership evaluation | Expands the literature considered while identifying which findings concern SAV/AV pilots or stated preferences rather than observed robotaxi demand |
| Final candidate-variable interpretation | Added a separate treatment of stated-choice/acceptance variables alongside observed robotaxi and conventional ride-hailing evidence | Keeps the evidence categories distinct when moving from literature review to candidate-variable construction |
| Role in the optimization model | Clarified that fare and waiting time belong to a decision-dependent extension, while survey constructs are not directly measurable exogenous city covariates | Prevents mixing operator decisions, latent attitudes, and city characteristics in the same exogenous demand-regression role |

These additions are identified by the archive's dated revision markers. No pre-addition snapshot is retained here, so an exact sentence-by-sentence earlier version cannot be reconstructed.

# Demand-evidence review: revision history

This log distinguishes additions explicitly marked in the 2026-10-01 archive from differences between that archive and the current working review. No earlier pre-addition file is preserved here, so the marked additions are not presented as a reconstructed full version diff.

## Additions marked on 2026-10-01

| Part marked as added | Content / change | Purpose supported by the text |
|---|---|---|
| Stated-choice and acceptance studies | Adds discussions of fare, waiting time, support features, acceptance constructs, and usage experience | Explains possible choice mechanisms while distinguishing them from observed demand and directly measurable city covariates |
| Additional SAV/AV papers | Adds papers 8–12 on adoption, preferences, reviews, and actual pilot ridership experiences | Broadens the evidence base while retaining application and measurement limitations; SAV/AV evidence is not automatically robotaxi market-share evidence |
| Final interpretation of choice/acceptance variables | Adds a separate discussion that service attributes and survey constructs are not exogenous city context; fare/waiting-time effects belong to a decision-dependent extension | Prevents treating operator-controlled attributes or attitude scores as directly observed city-level demand drivers |

These are additions identified by the archive's own date labels. The labels do not establish that every sentence in the marked sections was newly written that day.

# Background-research history

## Archived demand-evidence review

The 2026-10-01 snapshot organizes seven demand/choice papers into three evidence levels:

1. Observed robotaxi trips: direct but geographically limited evidence from San Francisco.
2. Conventional ride-hailing: indirect evidence for scale, urban form, and accessibility.
3. Stated preference and acceptance: evidence about choice mechanisms rather than observed robotaxi demand.

It pairs candidate variables with comparable measurement sources such as ACS, SLD, and trip records. A measured variable is not automatically a causal factor, and an association with trips does not identify an effect on $p$ alone.

This is a dated research record, not a separate experiment or an asserted list of changes. For the working review and final variable decisions, use the sibling `1) Research` and the selection folder.
